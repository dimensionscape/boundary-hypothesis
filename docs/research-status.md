# Research status

The manuscript proposes that all physical actuality ultimately originates without antecedent actuality. Absolute nothingness denotes no positive actuality, rather than an empty physical system. The hypothesis assigns explanatory significance to the absence of antecedent restrictions. Properties and relations established with actuality then constrain its further development.

## Established within the models

- A declared property profile determines a represented response exactly when equal profiles have equal responses.
- A finite fermionic gain construction closes its specified addition channels while allowing continuing internal dynamics. Its count law and closure-time distribution are explicit.
- An infinite family of candidate labels can have finite compatible occupancy under a bounded attribute range and positive exclusion scale.
- Persistent accessibility gives almost-sure closure in the packing model, including specified biased and history-dependent exploration. Failure examples show why the assumptions matter.
- Bounded residual intensity, effects, and amplification give a finite-horizon operational-stability estimate.
- Persistent past response restricts possible count histories under stated assumptions. Different finite-start and beginningless histories can nevertheless have identical represented futures when past rates vary.

These are conditional mathematical results. Their proofs and assumptions appear in the paper. Computational checks supplement the proofs.

## Open questions

The key origin step is H3: whether positive actuality can obtain with no antecedent actuality. Removing restrictions does not by itself prove realization, and the mathematical examples begin with positive structure.

The benchmark response laws, capacities, and parameters also require physical motivation. The examples show how constraints can depend on structure, but do not derive the particular laws or constants of our universe.

A cosmological version would need physical degrees of freedom, a spacetime or precursor interpretation, a consistent treatment of conservation, an appropriate continuum limit, and a discriminating observational consequence. The paper's cosmological equations specify matching requirements rather than an empirical fit or a derived Big Bang.

## Scope of this release

Version 1.0.0 contains the consolidated manuscript and calculations directly relevant to it. Earlier working drafts and abandoned reformulations are outside this release. The release does not claim external peer review or experimental confirmation.
